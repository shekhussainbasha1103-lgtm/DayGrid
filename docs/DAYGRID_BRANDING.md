# Daygrid — app branding

## Name
Daygrid

## Tagline
Your day. Your campus. Your flow.

## Brand meaning
A clear, flexible grid for organizing student life: classes, attendance, meals, campus navigation, notices, deadlines and personal goals.

## Visual direction
- Futuristic student-life companion with comic-inspired accents.
- Dark and light themes, rounded cards, icon-led navigation and customizable accent colors.
- Suggested signature palette: deep midnight violet, electric lavender and cyan highlights.
- Use a distinctive illustrated campus/clock mascot in a future icon, with readable typography and accessible contrast.
- Keep critical information (attendance, room, deadlines and marks) clear and readable, even when decorative elements are used.

## Store description draft
Daygrid helps students organize campus life in one place: daily timetable, attendance goals, classroom details, mess menu, academic reminders, study plans and student-service shortcuts. Live academic records require authorized integration with the institution's systems.
